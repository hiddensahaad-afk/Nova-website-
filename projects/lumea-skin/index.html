A few things you might be wondering

Good to know.

Still have a question? We'd love to hear from you.

Where should I start with my routine?

Keep it simple: cleanse, choose a treatment if it suits your needs, and moisturize. In the morning, finish with a broad-spectrum sunscreen. Introduce new products gradually and follow their label directions.

Can I use LUMÉA products with my current routine?

Many people pair basic skincare with products they already use. Check each product's ingredient list, introduce one new item at a time, and ask a qualified skincare professional if you are unsure what is right for you.

Do I need sunscreen in the morning?

Daily broad-spectrum sun protection is an important part of a morning skincare routine. Follow the sunscreen label for application and reapplication guidance.

How can I get help with an order?

Our customer care team is happy to help. Email hello@lumeaskin.com and include your order details if you have them.
