Good to know

Questions, answered.

Clear expectations make for better creative partnerships.

What book promotion services do you offer?

Cinematic promotional video concepts, editorial articles, social media content, book graphics, author branding, launch campaigns, and promotional copy.

Can you create a cinematic video for my book?

Yes. We can create a promotional storytelling asset shaped around your book's atmosphere and genre. Specific formats and deliverables are agreed before work begins.

Can you write an article for Medium?

We can prepare an editorial-style article for Medium or another suitable platform. Publication acceptance, reach, indexing, and engagement are subject to platform rules and audience response.

Do you promote books on social media?

We create social graphics, captions, short-form video concepts, and content plans. Posting and platform account access are included only when specifically agreed.

Can you work with self-published authors?

Yes. Services are available to independent, self-published, emerging, and established authors.

Can I combine several services?

Yes. Custom campaigns can combine services that suit your title, audience, goals, and budget, subject to review and agreement.

Do you guarantee book sales or bestseller rankings?

No. Sales, rankings, reach, and audience response vary. No specific result is guaranteed.

How long does a project take?

Timing depends on the agreed deliverables and scope. A timeline is confirmed before work begins.

Can I request revisions?

Revision rounds and what they cover are agreed as part of the project scope before payment.

What materials should I provide?

Usually your book title, description, genre, approved cover and images, book links, target audience, and goals. Please only share materials you have permission to use.

Do you publish articles directly to Medium?

Only when publishing assistance is included and agreed. Publication is subject to the platform's rules and acceptance.

Can you promote a book that has already been released?

Yes. We can discuss a fresh spotlight or campaign for an existing title.
