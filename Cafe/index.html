Roast & Crumb started with a simple belief: a good cup of coffee can change the shape of a day. We pair carefully prepared drinks with a kitchen that keeps the oven warm, creating a place that feels considered without ever feeling formal.

Come in for the morning light, stay for one more chapter, one more conversation, or one more crumb.

A small beginning

From the first pour
to the last crumb.

Roast & Crumb began as a shared table and a well-used espresso machine. Today, the heart is still the same: make the coffee carefully, bake with good ingredients, and leave enough time for people to feel at home.

Our menu changes with the seasons, our playlist changes with the room, and the door stays open for anyone who needs a warm drink and a little breathing space.
